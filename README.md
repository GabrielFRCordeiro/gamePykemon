> The Pykemon Project is the recreation of Pokemon's first battle in Python as a fun way to study the language

## 💻 How to Play

> [!NOTE]
> Choose your starter.

> [!NOTE]
> Battle against your rival.

> [!WARNING]
> Be careful with super-effective moves.

> [!TIP]
> Find the hidden starter on the starter selection screen.
